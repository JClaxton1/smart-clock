How to use it

Everything works with a wave of your hand or from your phone. Nothing here can break it.

Waving at it

There's a small window in the wood just below the hands. Bring your hand toward it, a few centimetres away, then take it away again. You don't need to touch anything.

Do thisAnd it will
TapTurn the light on or off. A single tap takes about a second to register, because the clock waits to see if a second tap is coming.
Tap twiceSwitch to the next light style.
Hold 2 secondsShow today's weather on the little screen for 20 seconds.
Hold 5 secondsShow a code on the screen for opening the settings page on your phone.

For a few seconds each minute a clock hand passes right over the window, and the clock can't see your hand through it. If a wave doesn't work, wait a moment and try again. After the clock restarts it spends about a minute learning where its hands pass, so gestures start working after that.

You can change what each gesture does on the Gestures tab of the settings page.

The settings page

Hold your hand over the window for 5 seconds. A square code appears on the little screen. Point your phone's camera at it and tap the link that pops up. The code also shows for the first minute after the clock starts. Your phone has to be on the same WiFi as the clock.

Changes save as you make them. There are four tabs:

Light

Turn the ring on or off, choose a style and a colour, set the brightness, and choose how long it stays on before turning itself off. Quiet at night keeps it dark between the times you pick. Hour chime sends a little sparkle round the ring on the hour while the light is on. Breathing guide has a Start button.

Gestures

Shows the last thing the clock saw, so you can practise. Tap a gesture to change what it does: it can turn the light on or off, change style, change brightness, show the weather, show the settings code, or start the breathing guide.

Clock

Your messages (send a heart back, mark them as seen, or clear them) and your reminders (add, turn off or delete). Below those, the hands: what they should say and whether they're on time. If they ever look wrong, use Hands showing the wrong time? and tell it what they show; it works out the rest. Under Keeping them in sync, the clock uses the little window to watch the second hand go by, and quietly fixes any tiny slip of the hands. Then the screen settings: °F or °C, the rain warning, and your zip code for the weather.

Setup

Things that were set when the clock was made, plus the clock's software version, a Restart the clock button and the WiFi settings. They're locked so nothing changes by accident. Tap Unlock to change them; they lock again when you leave the tab.

The little screen

The time, the date, and the temperature and humidity in the room. When rain is on the way, the humidity corner shows the time it's likely to start instead. It's e-paper, like an e-reader, so it uses no power to keep showing a picture and a short flicker once an hour is normal. You can switch between °F and °C on the Clock tab.

Messages

Sometimes a little message arrives. It appears on the small screen with a heart at the top, and the ring glows a soft heartbeat so you notice it. The heartbeat stays quiet during night hours and starts again in the morning, or when you switch the room lights on. It also waits while a clock hand is covering the little window, because then the clock can't see your wave.

Do thisAnd it will
WaveLet the sender know you've seen it, and stop the heartbeat.
TapSend a heart back.
Tap twiceShow the next new message, or go back to the clock.

A message you've seen goes away by itself after a minute, and the clock face comes back. The last few messages are also on the Clock tab of the settings page, with buttons to send a heart back, mark them as seen, or clear them all.

Reminders

On the settings page, open the Clock tab and tap Add a reminder. Type what to remember, pick a time, and choose once, every day, weekdays, weekends or particular days. When it's due, the little screen shows it and the ring glows gold, even during quiet hours, because you asked for it.

Do thisAnd it will
WaveFinish the reminder.
Tap twiceSnooze it for 10 minutes.

Rain warning

If rain is likely in the next few hours, the bottom-right corner of the screen says Rain at and the hour, instead of the humidity, and the ring shimmers blue once. You can switch this off on the Clock tab.

Breathing guide

For a calm minute and a half, tap Start next to Breathing guide on the Light tab, or set a gesture to start it. The ring slowly fills up both sides as you breathe in, stays full while you hold, and drains as you breathe out, six times. The screen shows each step. Wave to stop at any time.

Power

Keep the clock plugged in with its USB cable. The top of the settings page always shows the battery: charging, full, or how much is left and roughly how long it will last. If the power goes out, a battery inside keeps the hands moving, and the little screen shows a small battery with its charge where it usually says Inside. If the battery starts getting low, the light stops first so the hands can keep going, and it comes back when the power does. If the battery runs very low, the hands park and the screen asks for the cable; once it's plugged in again they catch up by themselves.

New software

Now and then the clock gets new software over WiFi, around 3 in the morning. You don't need to do anything. If a new version ever misbehaves, the clock goes back to the previous one by itself.

If something looks wrong

The hands show the wrong time
Right after a power cut they may be catching up or waiting for the real time to catch up with them; give it a few minutes. If they're still wrong, open the Clock tab and use Hands showing the wrong time? or the 1-second buttons. Once they're right, the clock spends about 10 minutes watching the second hand go past the little window, and after that it keeps them right by itself.
The light doesn't answer a wave
Wait a minute if the clock just restarted. Bring your hand closer, about 3 to 5 centimetres from the window, and avoid the moment a hand is passing over it. If it's night, check Quiet at night on the Light tab.
The settings page won't open
Make sure your phone is on the home WiFi, not mobile data. Then hold a hand over the window for 5 seconds for a fresh code.
The WiFi name or password changed
Setup tab → Forget WiFi and set up again. The screen then shows a code to join a network called SmartClock-Setup; join it and choose the new WiFi. If you can't reach the settings page any more, unplug the clock for a few seconds and plug it back in: when it can't find its WiFi, it shows the setup code by itself.
Anything else
Setup tab → Restart the clock. It takes about 30 seconds, and the hands keep their place.
