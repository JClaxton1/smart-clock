Amanda's Clock

Real hands that sweep, a ring of light that answers a wave, and a little screen that knows the weather.

What it does

  • Keeps perfect time by itselfIt sets its own hands from the internet, handles daylight saving, and catches up on its own after a power cut.
  • Lights up when you waveBring a hand toward the little window under the hands and the ring glows. It turns itself off again after a while.
  • Shows the day at a glanceThe small screen shows the time, the date, and how warm and humid the room is. Hold a hand there for the weather outside.
  • Changes its moodTwelve light styles, any colour you like, and a quiet setting so it stays dark while you sleep.
  • Looks after itselfNew software arrives by itself at night. If anything ever goes wrong it puts itself back the way it was.
  • Keeps going in a power cutA battery inside keeps the hands moving when it's unplugged, and saves the light for when the power is back.
  • Brings little messagesA note from someone who loves you appears on the screen, and the ring glows a soft heartbeat until you see it. Tap to send a heart back.
  • Remembers things for youSet reminders on your phone, once or on repeat. When one is due the screen shows it and the ring glows gold.
  • Knows when rain is comingIf rain is likely in the next few hours, the screen tells you when, before you head out.
  • Helps you slow downA breathing guide: the ring rises and falls for a calm minute and a half.
